# Changelog

## 0.3.0 - 2026-10-12

### Changed

- Show selected cats and dogs beside their chairs in the Cabin by default.
- Change the host's Cabin-pets setting in Mod Settings for the next round.

### Fixed

- Keep the None pet selection and preserve Cabin pets after the opening transition.

## 0.2.0 - 2026-10-11

- Show everyone's pets beside their chairs in the Cabin by default, with a host setting to turn it off.
- Fix the selected material for dog skin 3.

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-11

- Show lobby members' selected cats and dogs with their own skins.
- Update pets when their owner changes the selection.
- Remove a player's extra pets when they leave the lobby.
